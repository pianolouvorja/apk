### EN
LouvorJA PIANO is the media operator app for Seventh-day Adventist worship services. Access official hymnals, play hymns with vocal or instrumental audio, follow Bible reading and organize the order of service — all in one place.

FEATURES

• Complete Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal and Louvor JA collections
• Vocal or instrumental audio playback
• Mini player: music continues while you switch tabs
• Global search: find any hymn or verse in seconds
• Full Bible with book, chapter and verse navigation
• Order of service with items and timer
• Service timer with stopwatch and countdown
• Offline downloads (single hymns or full albums)
• Light/dark theme and 10 accent colors
• Portuguese and Spanish
• Automatic updates with release notes

NO ADS. NO SIGN-UP. NO DATA COLLECTION.
